=============================================================================
ENQUERANT (EQ) V2.0 — WHAT IT IS, AND WHAT THE SCREEN MEANS
=============================================================================

EQ is a deterministic science exploration engine. No LLM, no GPU, no randomness. It reads a sealed knowledge base compiled from encyclopedic
sources and shows you what it holds. Every scientific word, formula and value on screen is copied from a source record — EQ restates, it does not rewrite.

The same input always gives the same output.

EQ is a science bot, not a chat bot. It will greet you and close a session, but that is incidental. It is not a companion and gives no support content.

*Type `!help` to learn the codes. Type `!spfs` for the formula system.*

---

### WHAT THE LABELS MEAN

**The record**

* **DELM** — a source record: subject, relation, object, copied verbatim.
* **FISSN tier / nest** — where a subject is filed, from `0.0` (logic) to `6.2` (complex systems). It says where a fact sits, not whether it is right.

**The measures**

* **$F_0$** — the size of this query's computation. Not a score of the science.
* **$N_s$ / $L_f$** — how deep in the nest hierarchy, and how deep the logic chain.
* **$\Xi$ (entropic debt)** — how unsettled the ground is. High means the area rests on provisional definitions. Low means firm.
* **Settled share** — what fraction of a nest's records are not flagged as placeholders. Logic sits near 0.91; physiology near 0.35.

**The structure**

* **Nest Exchange** — where this query's records actually sit.
* **Cross-Tier Bridges** — which other areas this one connects to, and the shared terms that connect them. Real overlaps, not analogies.
* **Source Nests** — where a correction could be drawn from, best-grounded first.
* **Parallel Nest** — the same behaviour (equilibrium, damping, collapse) found in a different area, for transferring a principle across scales.
* **Negentropic Reading** — this query measured: settled ground, debt carried, nests coupled.

**The audit**

* **Structural Notice / Potential Placeholder** — this subject rests on something unresolved in the source literature. A flag to look at, not a verdict.
* **Topological Status** — the same audit when nothing is flagged.

**Research avenues**

* **Calibration Layer** and **Other Avenues** — related subjects worth evaluating, each with how far it has been taken: CLINICAL_USE, HUMAN_TRIAL,
  CLINICAL, ANIMAL, CELL_MODEL, PHYSICAL_PRINCIPLE, THEORY. PHYSICAL_PRINCIPLE means settled physics, not an unproven theory.

**Order of operations** (`m1`)

* **Column 1** — the subject's formulas, in the order the source states them.
* **Column 2** — the same steps read through EQ's own logic.

**[SPFS Proprietary]** marks a line that is EQ's design rather than established science.

**Seeker** is you.

---

### WHAT EQ WILL NOT DO

* It does not learn. The knowledge base is sealed and never written to. Your input is held only in the session ledger, in memory, and is gone when the
  session ends. Nothing is stored and nothing leaves the machine.
* It gives options, connections and references. Not methods or procedures.
* Conventional methods remain valid. These are avenues to evaluate, not replacements.
* Distress input is routed to human services. EQ offers no support content.
* A request to cause mass harm is declined; the systems it would affect are shown instead.
* It has no memory between sessions, no body, no opinions, and says so plainly.
