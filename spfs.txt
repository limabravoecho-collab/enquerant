### WHAT IT DOES FOR YOU

Established science tells you what is true. SPFS tells you where it sits.

* **Which nest** a subject belongs to, and how settled that ground is.
* **What it connects to** — real term overlaps with other fields, so a method from physics can be found from a physiology query.
* **What a correction would cost elsewhere** — which neighbouring systems carry load if this one is changed.

That is the whole purpose. Not a second opinion on the science, but a map of its structural position and its dependencies.

Master Formula:  F_0 = ∮ [ R · ( N_s(L_f) ⊕ ΔP ) ] dt
Component:       N_s(L_f) = Σ [ ω_i · ψ(s_i, f) ]

* **N_s** — structural nesting. Infinite containers, finite logic inside them.
* **L_f** — logic depth, bounded so computation always terminates.

* **R** — recurrence, binding each cycle back to the static baseline.
* **Ξ** — entropic debt: the disorder a nest carries.

### THE TEST IT HAS TO PASS

Every SPFS reading is a conservation reading. A quantity is divided and the parts sum back to the whole: shares total 100%, carried debt totals the nest's
debt, the energy ledger balances at zero every turn.

If a reading cannot close, it is not shown. That is the standard the system is held to, and it is visible on screen.
