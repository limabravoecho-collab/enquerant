# ENQUERANT (EQ) V2.0 — HOW TO USE
=============================================================================

EQ is a science exploration engine. Ask about a subject and it shows you the records it holds, where that subject sits, and what it connects to.

*Type `!about` for what the screen labels mean. Type `!spfs` for the formula system.*

---

### ASK

Type the subject plainly.

* **Best:** the subject on its own — "histotripsy", "dark energy", "Riemann zeta function"
* **Fine:** a direct question — "what is dark energy?"
* **Avoid:** long framing around a short subject. Extra words dilute the match.

If EQ does not hold a subject, it shows the nearest records it has. It will not invent an answer. If a word is misspelled, it offers the closest matches as a
numbered list.

---

### NAVIGATE

Results come eight at a time.

* `1`–`8` — open that record
* `000` — next eight
* `0` — back one step
* `00` — start over

Extra codes appear when there is something to use them on:

* `a1`–`a10` — a related research avenue
* `m1`, `m2` — the formulas for the subject you opened, 24 at a time

EQ tells you which codes are available at the bottom of each screen. If a code does nothing, it was not offered.

---

### TWO OTHER LISTS

* **Tier list** — the subject spans several areas. Pick the one you meant.
* **Topic offer** — you mentioned a subject in passing. Enter `1` or say yes.

---

### THE REST

* `!help` `!about` `!spfs` — these files
* The **COPY** button copies the whole session, formulas included, ready to paste into your own work.
* Ask "who are you" for what EQ is. Ask "how are you" and it will tell you it is in equilibrium, because it always is.

EQ has no memory between sessions and nothing is sent anywhere.
